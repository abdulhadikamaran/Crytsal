Features

Designed to disappear.

Crystal stays invisible until the moment you need it.

⚡

Lightning Fast

Powered by Groq's ultra-fast Llama models. Most corrections finish in under 500ms — faster than you can look away from the screen.

Smart Key Rotation

Add up to 5 API keys. If one hits its rate limit, Crystal silently switches to the next one. Zero interruptions, ever.

🧠

Context-Aware AI

Two intelligent modes: a fast conservative model for simple fixes, and a powerful 70B model that understands technical jargon and complex sentences.

Not happy with the result? Press Shift + Q to instantly revert to your original text exactly as you wrote it.

Works Everywhere

Text inputs, textareas, Google Docs, Notion, Twitter, Gmail — if you can type in it, Crystal can fix it.

Highlight one sentence or a paragraph and Crystal fixes only that part. Surgical precision when you don't want to touch the rest.
